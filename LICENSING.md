# Metrakron licensing

The current primary licence for Oliver-owned code, original assets and
documentation is **Oliver's Minecraft Mods Restricted License 1.0**, identified
as `LicenseRef-Oliver-Restricted-1.0`. The complete current terms are in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Separately licensed material is excluded from that scope.

## Earlier grants remain intact

This change does not revoke, replace or narrow rights already granted for copies
supplied under earlier terms. The public source at commit
[`7ad067999a6f72606bddedfc2172643b6450471f`](https://github.com/erydon-mod/metrakron/tree/7ad067999a6f72606bddedfc2172643b6450471f)
identified Metrakron code as MIT and Oliver-owned visual assets as CC BY-SA 4.0.
Those grants remain available for the material covered by them, including that
material when it is unchanged in the current tree. Their copyright, attribution
and other conditions still apply. They do not automatically cover later new
material or changes offered only under the current restricted licence.

The complete earlier MIT text and copyright notice are retained in
[`LICENSES/MIT-Previously-Granted.txt`](LICENSES/MIT-Previously-Granted.txt).
The complete CC BY-SA 4.0 text is retained in
[`LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`](LICENSES/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Attribution for the
previously licensed ERYDON-owned visual assets is ERYDON Metrakron by Oliver,
with source at <https://github.com/erydon-mod/metrakron>; identify adaptations
and follow the applicable licence conditions. Their provenance and scope are
recorded in [`ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`](ASSET_PROVENANCE.md) and in the earlier
commit's licence map. Already published artefacts and their licence notices
are unchanged.

## Separately licensed material

Cinzel, Lato and Space Mono fonts and their generated bitmap atlases retain
their SIL Open Font License 1.1 terms. The Gradle wrapper retains its upstream
licence. Full attribution, paths and licence texts are listed in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). These terms and notices
are not replaced by the current primary licence.
